```
🚀 DevGotchi 2.1 is live — and this update is about making the game more honest.

DevGotchi turns VS Code into an RPG: your stats (Focus, Motivation, Energy,
Health) decay as you work, real coding activity — saves, commits, bug fixes —
earns XP, and a developer avatar reacts to your session in real time. The

2.1 ties the game closer to signals that are already true about your work,

The panel has always had a boss card. Now, whenever you have active lint or

you're rewarded. No errors, and the card reverts to the original

⏱️ FOCUS SPRINT
A genuine Pomodoro-style timer (15/25/50 minutes) with a 1.5x XP multiplier

Once a week, a quiet notification summarizes what actually changed — XP,
commits, bugs fixed, sprints completed, streak, level. If you had a quiet

makes people feel bad for resting.

A new export feature: copy your stats as a Markdown block for a GitHub

client-side in the panel. Both are fully local — the extension has zero
telemetry, so nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it.
```

## Notes on cross-posting

- Attach `devgotchi_boss_fight.gif` wherever the platform supports native
  video/GIF upload (X, Facebook, Instagram) rather than linking to it —
  autoplaying media in-feed gets dramatically more reach than a link-out.
- LinkedIn doesn't render GIFs as well in-feed as the other three; a static
  screenshot or a short native video clip performs better there. If you
  want video for LinkedIn specifically, converting the GIF to an MP4 loop
  is worth doing.
- Stagger these by a few hours to a day rather than all at once — some
  people follow you on more than one platform, and identical simultaneous
  posts read as obviously cross-posted/automated.
- Same rule as always: reply to early comments fast. The algorithm reward
  for engagement velocity in the first hour is bigger than almost anything
  else you can do after hitting publish.
